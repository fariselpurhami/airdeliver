History: PR3 introduced catalog-clause rendering in ce49f8c3af5cfef484246a6999c4ff0f1d660eac and applied it to welcome rows in a8ffbe1f347c6f98cf2d90a1272ccf39b0589ee3. Its second section could be generated by the model using a lifestyle/synergy prompt. Thus the original was a mixture of fixed fallback and generated prose, not a single recoverable static paragraph.

This patch restores the two-section considered-editorial presentation, not unsupported exact assertions. A deterministic row template draws only from the actual sanitized displayed items, their names/categories/brands and known same-currency prices. No invented brand, popularity, personalized standards, material benefits, skin effect, delivery or item not in the cards. Missing prices omit the price range. No free generated lifestyle text. This is deliberately labeled a template in the implementation, not a model-generated claim. Product selection/search logic stays unchanged. Existing general recommendation factual renderer and bounded editorial validator remain for their other callers. Opening-row rendering no longer needs a model call.
