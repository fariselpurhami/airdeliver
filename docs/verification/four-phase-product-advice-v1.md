# Product advice: original four logical phases

The shared sales response boundary now asks the writer and independent reviewer
for opening, data core, synthesis and closure on substantive catalog advice.
Price inquiry, clarification, social/no-match and the existing two-paragraph
opening-carousel contract retain their shorter shapes. Card snapshots, exact
field validation, independent review, synchronous flow, timeouts and quotas are
unchanged. Old persona/system prompts are preserved, not reactivated. No fake
global availability, private knowledge, superiority or procurement promises.

This is a model policy and independent semantic review, not a deterministic
natural-language quality guarantee. Controlled tests establish policy delivery,
exceptions, review withholding and caller routing, not live model compliance.
The canonical twenty partial groups are not promoted by this change.

owner-machine execution, migrations on owner data, deployment or spending.
